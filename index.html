CHAPTER 01

The Day We First Meet

It was just an ordinary day... until suddenly, the rain came. I still remember that moment — the tiny drops of rain falling on your face, resting on your glasses, and somehow making everything around you feel a little more beautiful. Maybe it was just a rainy day for everyone else. But for me, it became one of those moments I knew I would never forget. That was the day a beautiful memory quietly became a part of our story.

CHAPTER 02

The First Date

I still remember you that day. You looked so beautiful — the kind of beautiful I know I will remember forever. But that day wasn't just a date for me. It felt like a gift. The first time I held your hand, everything around me seemed to disappear. And when you came into my arms on that boat... for a moment, my mind became quiet, and my heart finally felt at peace. Just you. Just me. The water around us. And a moment I wish I could live one more time. That wasn't simply our first date. That was one of the most beautiful gifts life ever gave me. 🤍

CHAPTER 03

Today, I don't feel the need for anything else. I just want you. In my heart, there is a place that belongs only to you — a place no one else could ever replace. You are not just a beautiful chapter in my story. You are the person I want beside me for all the chapters that are still waiting to be written. My favorite person. My forever home. My wife, forever in my heart. No matter how much time passes, that place will always be yours. Until my very last breath, I will carry this love with me. 🤍
